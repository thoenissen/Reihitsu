## New principle candidates

- P14 (candidate): boundary of P9 and P10. P10 governs the changed node: its own tokens, indentation and line starts, plus the whitespace on the lines the fix edited. P9 governs everything beyond: gaps to a neighbor (blank lines before the node, the text after its closing brace, `} int _x;`) belong to the neighbor's rule. Decides #1209 Q3, #1220 Q2, #1169 Q1 bullet 5 (no blank line inserted) and #1170 Q1 reading (b); #1168 Q1 and #1209 Q4 stay P10. Alternative: the maintainer decides each of these four separately.
- P15 (candidate): a diagnostic always has a source location (a rule does not report what has no identifier or tokens to point at, or it reports at a defined token such as the `extension` keyword). Rests on #1127 (decided: bug) and decides #1221 Q1's location part. Report-or-not for extension blocks stays with the maintainer.
- P16 (candidate): a directive inside the construct a rule reports. Conditional directives and excluded code make the rule not report (as SHARED-TRAIL-002); region and pragma directives do not. Decides #1169 Q3 (reading b) and keeps the trailing-comma and empty-type rules aligned; related #1240.
- Recurring pattern worth a note to the squads: every fix that delegates to `FormatNodeInDocumentAsync` has the P9, P10 and G6 exposure; a shared fix base that formats only the changed node and its own gaps would close G1, G2 and G6 at once (#1140, #1143, #1144).
