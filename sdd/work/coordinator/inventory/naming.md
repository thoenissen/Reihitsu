## Cross-issue duplicates

- **Metadata-dictated member names (B):** #1013 Q1 (methods), #1014 Q1 (events), #1025 Q1 (public props), #1026 Q1 (protected props), #1027 Q1 (internal props) — one decision. #1023 Q1 (tuple element names in overrides) is the same "externally dictated" question without a fix; #1013 Q2 (P/Invoke) is the native-name variant.
- **Compile-error check scope (A):** #1009 Q1 (namespace-level types skip the check) and #1025/#1026/#1027 (check only looks at the renamed symbol's containing type) both concern SHARED-CASING-009 not catching added errors.
- **`@` handling (C):** #1011 Q1 and #1012 Q2 (comparison includes `@`); #1009 Q2 (fix produces `@keyword`).
- **Prefix doubling:** #1010 Q1 (`I`) and #1020 Q1 (`T`).
- **Deconstruction ownership:** #1019 Q3 and Q4 should be decided together.

## NEW patterns

- **NEW:prefix-doubling** — fix for a required prefix ignores an existing prefix (with `_` or lowercase) and adds a second one: #1010 Q1, #1020 Q1.
- **NEW:cross-rule-consistency** — a rule reports, exempts, or owns a construct differently from sibling rules for an equivalent construct (coverage/ownership gaps): #1019 Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4; #1022 Q1; #1024 Q1.
