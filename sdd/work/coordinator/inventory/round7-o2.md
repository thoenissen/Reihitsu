## New principle candidates

- **P14 (candidate): an element's regions belong to the innermost enclosing type-like declaration (class, struct, interface, record, enum, delegate-less), and regions of different declarations are never compared or moved across.** Decides #1185 Q2, #1187 Q2 and, by extension, supports #1183 Q1 (innermost region judges a member) if the maintainer wants the same nesting rule for regions.
- **P15 (candidate): rules that compare a sequence compare against the previous element of the same group regardless of what else lies between** (as RH7207 handles contiguity separately). Decides #1171–#1173; would also apply to other order rules (RH7101–RH7110 ordering) where an unrelated member between hides an out-of-order pair.
- **P9/P10 interaction (clarification needed):** blank lines that remain after a removed directive. Proposed reading: the blank line created or left by removing a line the diagnostic reports is part of that fix's changed code, so P10 applies (laid out as the formatter lays it out); blank lines elsewhere stay untouched (P9). Would fully decide #1175 Q4 and #1186 Q1 as one bug.
- **Mutually exclusive `#if` branches (candidate, from #1187 Q1):** needs a general decision for rules that look for duplicates/uniqueness across a type (RH7311, possibly duplicate-member style rules); not yet a principle.
- **Hint:** no existing principle P1–P13 fully decides any of the 8 undecided single questions; 9 of 21 are already decided by a principle named in the issue itself (#1174 Q1, #1175 Q1/Q2/Q3, #1176 Q1, #1177 Q1b, #1185 Q3, plus fix parts of #1176 Q2, #1185 Q2).
