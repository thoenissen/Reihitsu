## Architecture findings

**The node-level column step and the indentation phase both decide the final column of a line that starts inside a comment, and of every line aligned to an anchor on it.**

- **Components:**
  - `ReihitsuFormatterHelpers.ComputeTokenColumn` and `AdjustNodeIndentation`, the step after the pipeline in `FormatNodeInDocumentAsync` and `FormatNodeInDocumentWithContextAsync`;
  - `IndentationPhase`, through `LayoutComputer.GetColumnBeforeRawStringShift` and `TryGetLineTextColumn`.
- **Shared decision.** The indentation phase now measures an anchor behind the last line of a multi-line comment where it stands. The node-level step then moves every other line by the target's column offset, but not that line.
- **Relation to earlier work.** This is the class of 08-implementer's architecture finding. Decision `2026-10-25-phase-prediction-removal-node-level-shift` chose a narrow fix, which covers only a complex-element brace in the RH5303 and RH5304 fixes. The members below were not part of that question. RH5303-006's own *Why* states the mechanism for any line measured from the comment-tail line.

Failing members at head. The comment `/* a` / `b */` stands in front of the anchor in every row, and the outer braces are misplaced so that the rule reports.
