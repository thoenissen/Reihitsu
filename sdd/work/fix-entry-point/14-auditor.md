## Architecture findings

- **Components.** Three components decide whether the gap between two members holds a line break:
  - `MemberLineStartRewriter`, through `TokenGapUtilities.HasLineBreakBetween` (`TokenGapAnalysis.HasLineBreak`). This is the line-break phase's owner of "every member starts its own line".
  - `FieldDeclarationSplitTransform.BuildTrailingTrivia`, which predicts which gaps the line-break phase will break and writes a break only where it predicts none: behind a `//` comment, and in front of a leading comment.
  - `ReihitsuFormatter.FormatSplitFieldInContext`, which decides for itself, through `SyntaxTriviaUtilities.AreSeparatedByEndOfLine`, which neighbouring member shares the target's line and must start the next one.
- **Decision they share.** Whether a member starts its own line, and so whether a field the split creates, or a member next to it, needs a line break written in front of it.
- **Input that shows them disagreeing.** A multi-line `/* */` comment that trails a separating comma (#1), or that trails the token in front of a shared-line gap (#2). The rewriter counts its inner line break, the transform's kind check misses it, and `AreSeparatedByEndOfLine` does not count it.
- **Structural cause.** The transform predicts another phase's behavior. The entry point re-decides a line-break-phase decision with a different predicate.
- **Scope.** All three components are in the run (the transform and `ReihitsuFormatter` change; the rewriter is their counterpart).
