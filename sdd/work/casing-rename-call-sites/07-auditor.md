## Architecture findings
Finding #1 has a structural cause of the kind `squad/README.md`, *Architecture findings*, names: a shared helper and a private copy of it. `GetOldNames` (`CasingCodeFixProviderBase{T}.cs:403`) is the owner of "the names the cascade gives up". It already excludes spellings the cascade still carries after the rename, and symbols the renamer leaves as they are (its remarks name the partial-method type parameter). `RewritesOtherCodeAsync` (`:1041`) builds its own set from every name the cascade reaches. Inputs c4 and d1 show the two disagreeing. The defect is of the run's issue.
