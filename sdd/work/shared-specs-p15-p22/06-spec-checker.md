## Answer to the open point (RH5111-005 and RH3001-006 against SHARED-DIAG-008)

SHARED-DIAG-008 defines the judged span as "the part of the construct its verdict is about". Its listed instances are all gaps and lists: a modifier list, a declarator list, an accessor list, the gap between a trailing comma and the closing brace, a namespace header.

**RH5111, `#if` between the declarator name and `=`: yes, that gap is the judged span.**
- `RH5111AssignmentsMustHaveProperLineBreaksAnalyzer.CheckAssignment` reads exactly three line numbers: where the target ends, where the `=` starts, and where the value starts. Its verdict is the relation between those positions, so the gap between target and `=` is what it judges. It is the same kind of span as the comma gap in SHARED-TRAIL-002.
- The analyzer never inspects trivia. The shape in `VerifyDeclaratorWithDirectiveIsReportedWithoutCodeFix` (`#if !UNDEFINED_SYMBOL` / `#endif` between `value` and `= 1`) is reported today. The fix is withheld by `ContainsJoinBlockingTrivia` in the code-fix provider.
- SHARED-DIAG-008 says "a conditional directive ... or code that such a directive excludes". SHARED-TRAIL-002 and its test `VerifyCommaInFrontOfEmptyConditionalBlockIsNotReported` show that an empty `#if`/`#endif` counts. So the letter of DIAG-008 gives "not reported" for this shape.
- RH5111-005 is active and enforced. It says the fix is withheld "when a comment or a preprocessor directive sits inside the reported ... variable declarator" and "the diagnostic is still reported". The test is tagged `RH5111-005`.
- For a conditional directive in that gap, the two active requirements contradict each other. For `#region`, `#endregion`, `#pragma`, and comments they agree (reported, fix withheld).
- No follow-up issue tracks it. #1337 covers only RH7101 and RH7105 to RH7108.

**RH3001, `#if/#else/#endif` between `!` and the operand: no, the gap is not the judged span.**
- `RH3001NotOperatorShouldNotBeUsedAnalyzer` judges only `GetTypeInfo(node.Operand).Type` (is it `bool`) and reports on the `!` token. It never reads the trivia between `!` and the operand. Only the fix reads it (`HasSafeOperandTrivia`).
- In the pinned shape, `#if DEBUG` is the leading trivia of the operand token `true`. `#else`, the skipped `false`, and `#endif` are the leading trivia of the `;` token, after the `!x` expression ends.
- The operand node `true` contains no directive. So the pinned RH3001-006 shape (`DirectiveAfterTheOperatorIsReportedWithoutACodeFix`) is not an instance of DIAG-008 by its definition. I found no conflict for that shape.
- The second RH3001-006 shape is different: `DirectivesInsideTheParenthesizedOperandDoNotWithholdTheFix`. There `#if DEBUG`/`#else`/`#endif` lies inside the parentheses, inside the operand node whose type the analyzer judges. It is reported, and the fix is offered. DIAG-008's text does not say whether an operand expression whose type is judged counts as a "span". Its examples are lists and gaps. By the "why" clause (another configuration compiles another operand) it could. This is a reading question for the maintainer, not something the text settles. See finding 2.

Conclusion: RH5111 contradicts DIAG-008 and is untracked. RH3001-006 as pinned does not contradict it. The RH3001 parenthesized-operand shape is undecided.
