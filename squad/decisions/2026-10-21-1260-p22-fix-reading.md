## Question

SHARED-DIAG-008 (P22) says a `#region`, `#endregion` or `#pragma` inside the judged span keeps the report but withholds the fix. Today the RH5409 fix removes a trailing comma in front of such a directive and leaves the directive in place (SHARED-TRAIL-002 example, a SHARED-TRAIL-003 test). Options: A — the fix is always withheld (RH5409–RH5411 lose a safe fix, needs a behavior-change follow-up); B — the fix is withheld only when it would rewrite or move the part of the judged span that holds the directive.

## Answer

Reading B. A fix that leaves the directive and its line in place, such as removing the comma in front of it, stays offered.
