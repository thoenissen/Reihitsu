## Question

The spec author found that RH3201's documentation-line exception depends on what counts as a documentation line (`///` but not `////`), which no SHARED-DOC requirement states. ## Answer

RH3201 states the documentation-line test in its own requirement, and its `Uses` row is `—`. `shared/element-documentation.md` stays unchanged.
