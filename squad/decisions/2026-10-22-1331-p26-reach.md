## Question

SHARED-FIX-007's moved-code bullet (P26) says code a fix moves or wraps into a new block keeps its layout. Three enforced families lay such code out with the formatter today: (a) the brace fixes RH5405–RH5407 (SHARED-BRACE-002), (b) the expression-body-to-block fixes RH3202 and RH3203, (c) the using-block organizers RH7201–RH7207 (FMT-USING-002). Options: P26 covers (a) only; all three; none of them.

## Answer

P26 covers (a): wrapping an embedded statement in braces is the same shape as the RH7004 example of P26, so SHARED-BRACE-002 is a P26 instance, filed in the open-questions issue of `sdd/specs/shared/code-fixes.md` as decided by P26. It does not cover (b), where the expression becomes a new return statement (the replaced node of P10), or (c), where the group separators are the subject of the using rules. SHARED-FIX-007 gets a scope sentence stating this.
