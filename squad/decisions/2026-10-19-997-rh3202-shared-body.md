## Question

The new `shared/expression-body-conversion.md` (SHARED-BODY-001..003: the guard, the diagnostic span, the conversion through the formatter) states for properties, indexers, operators, conversion operators, and local functions what RH3202-002, RH3202-003, and RH3202-004 state for methods. Options: retire RH3202-002..004 and let RH3202 use SHARED-BODY; keep both statements without retiring anything; or drop the shared spec and let every rule restate the behavior.

## Answer

Retire RH3202-002, RH3202-003, and RH3202-004. RH3202 lists the SHARED-BODY requirements under `Uses`, and SHARED-BODY covers methods as well.
